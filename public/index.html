About Daily Rhythm

Daily Rhythm turns your day into a glanceable clock. See what you are doing now, what is next, prayer times, and the moon phase, while adding or changing several activities naturally in English or Arabic. The goal is to reduce planning friction and help you stay present in your day — not manage another complicated calendar.

Why you are seeing this

You opened the moon. Al-Biruni studied the sky, time and the Earth with mathematics and observation.

What he contributed

He wrote extensively on astronomy and devised a geometric method for estimating the Earth’s radius from observations made from a mountain.

Why it still matters

His work is a reminder that careful measurement can connect the sky above us with the world beneath our feet.
